# Lessons (append-only, never delete; mark superseded instead). Add with scripts/add_lesson.sh
1. [captions] Captions must come from exact word times, not guesses. Take word times from Piper's phoneme alignment (piper-tts include_alignments, audio/make_voice_v4.py), never whisper tiny.en. Show the real spoken words, 1-3 at a time, gone right after the last word ends. Hand-anchored regexes caused out-of-sync captions. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
2. [voice] No glued-in pauses (gap 0.06 s), about 3.5+ words/s, under 1 s of total dead air. Re-run sound cues and music after any timing change. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
3. [process] Use the skill's own look values (light presets, ink widths, DOF per shot). Do not invent a custom look and call it "following the skill". Colour per topic may change the tint, not the light model. Ep01's custom flat shader scored ~4/10. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
4. [light] Day or golden light for most scenes; night only for spooky ones. Golden at low sun turns floors violet: use day for interior or close-up shots. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
5. [light] Never pick a floor or road colour that is lilac or blue. Check one still per shot for a purple cast before showing anything. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
6. [world] A shot must show what its line says. A random floor object or meaningless yellow bars fails; the verifier checks line vs picture for every shot. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
7. [world] Every scene needs a foreground prop, a grounded subject with a contact shadow, and a filled background. No cropped faces at the frame edge unless deliberate. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
8. [process] Judge short rendered motion clips with a fresh reviewer each round, not stills judged by the builder (builders score ~2 points too high). Pass is 7+ per area. (research, 2026-11-26)
9. [process] Never claim "following the skill" without checking it against the skill's steps: measure the reference, owners per file, 8-area scoring. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
10. [process] Never run a GPU render on Andrew's PC without his "render" in his own words in the thread. (ep01, 2026-11-26)
11. [post] Contact shadows must use a multiply blob card (key contains |blob|); a soft card with a white background draws white squares under characters. (ep01 rebuild, 2026-11-27)
12. [world] Build sets from the skill template's world pieces (facade houses, painted ground, cumulus) before writing custom geometry; the template look comes from world construction, not post. The template world builder is bridge-only, so pieces must be ported. (ep01 rebuild, 2026-11-27)
13. [world] Textures and ink come from metre-scaled painted textures on every set surface (tileTex, plank courses), not flat colours; this moved the reviewer from 4.1 to 4.9. Long shots need clearly different angles, and a character must never be half cut off at the frame edge. (ep01 rebuild, 2026-11-27)
14. [process] Every thread must read upstream/SKILL.md and upstream/STYLE_BIBLE.md in full itself, never a summary, including script-writing threads. Past failures came from threads that only got summaries and treated scripts as outside the skill. (Andrew, 2026-11-26)
15. [script] Read the whole raw source before scripting; the script narrates the story, not the source thread; every line concrete and showable. (ep01, 2026-11-25)
16. [process] The coordinator checks real video frames against the references itself before showing Andrew; never pass on a thread's self-report unchecked. Show proof (side-by-side frames), make no promises. (Andrew, 2026-11-26)
