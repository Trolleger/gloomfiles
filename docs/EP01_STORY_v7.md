## Full script (as it will be read)
An anonymous man online says he can attack strangers with his mind, and he keeps score. It's October on 4chan's paranormal board, and he's blogging his psychic experiments: eight so far, and three, he says, worked. One reader calls that like hitting a one-metre target a kilometre away, in a storm. Another traces his method to a paperback, The Forbidden Parapsychology: attacks on strangers, "not meant to kill". Most people would brag about three out of eight, but he keeps writing about failure. Then he stops posting, everywhere. A week later, a thread named after Death Note, the manga where a written name kills, asks where he went. Wait, did real life catch up with him? Or did the glowies, slang for feds, find him? Then one reply says: I didn't think there were others like me. Another notices his blog takes anonymous comments. Luckily, anyone can write to him. Unluckily, anyone can write to him. The last post: I left him one. You see, back in 1942, Walter Cannon argued a curse could kill through fear alone. Ever heard a creak in a dark house and felt your heart pound before you knew why? But his three can't be checked, because he never said who, or what counted as a hit. And the man who wrote about every failure never wrote back.

**223 words.** At the skill's pace (3.7 words/s) that's about 60 s. At Joe's current measured pace (2.87 words/s) it's about 78 s, so Joe needs to read about 25% faster (Piper length_scale ~0.78, to test on the PC) or the script gets cut further.
