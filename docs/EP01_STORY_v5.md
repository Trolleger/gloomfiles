## Script (202 words, about 70 s at the fast Joe pace)
In October, on 4chan's paranormal board, an anonymous man was running what he called psychic experiments, and keeping score on his blog. Eight attempts. Three, he reported, worked. One reader said that was like hitting a one-metre target a kilometre away, in a storm. Another traced his method to a paperback, The Forbidden Parapsychology: psychic attacks on strangers, "not meant to kill". And that reader noticed something odd: he kept writing about failure, as if every attempt should have worked. Then he stopped writing at all. After a week of silence, someone started a thread asking where he'd gone, titled after Death Note, the manga where writing a name in a notebook kills. The guesses were ordinary: real life, boredom, or the glowies, slang for feds, had found him. Then, two hours in, the replies changed. One said they hadn't thought there were others like them. Another noticed his blog takes anonymous comments. The last post said only: I left him one. It's an old fear. In 1942, Walter Cannon argued that belief in a curse could kill through fear alone. But no one in that thread ever learned what a success was, or who the strangers were. Because he never replied.

Cut vs v4: the "no results" guess and Cannon's job title, to stay at 70 s. Next cuts for 60 s: "In October", the guesses line (about 5 s).
