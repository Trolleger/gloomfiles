# Ep 1 "Anon Went Quiet": script v9 (flowing narration). Awaiting Andrew's OK.

There's a man online who says he can curse strangers, and the odd part is that it only works when he stops trying. His targets, he says, get anything from a cold or a missed bus to, very rarely, death. His surest method is to print their photo, piss on it while saying their name, and wish them the worst without thinking, just pure anger.

Then he started running it like an experiment, with more than forty targets, each matched against a control he never touched, and all of them coded so nobody could say he'd changed the list. One reader saw three hits out of eight and compared it to hitting a one-metre target from a thousand metres, in a storm.

But after admitting that most attempts failed, he went quiet. A week later, in a thread named after Death Note, a stranger wrote, "I didn't think there were others like me," and someone else left him an anonymous comment. As far as that thread shows, he never answered.

Luckily, there's no proof he ever hurt anyone. Unluckily, there's no proof he didn't. And belief alone can be powerful, because back in 1942, a Harvard scientist named Walter Cannon argued that people who truly believe they're cursed could die of fear. That's the part that sticks, because this man said it only works when he stops trying, and now he seems to have stopped.
