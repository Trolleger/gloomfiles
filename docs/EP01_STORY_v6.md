## Script (210 words)
An anonymous man online says he can attack strangers with his mind, and three times out of eight, it works. It's October, on 4chan's paranormal board, and he's logging his psychic experiments on a blog. One reader says three of eight is like hitting a one-metre target a kilometre away, in a storm. Another traces his method to a paperback, The Forbidden Parapsychology: psychic attacks on strangers, "not meant to kill". But he isn't satisfied. He keeps writing about failure, as if every attempt should have worked. Then he stops posting. A week later, someone starts a thread asking where he went, and titles it after Death Note, the manga where a name in a notebook kills. Real life? Boredom? Or the glowies, slang for feds? You see, nobody ever said what counted as a success. It's like a weather forecaster who only tells you about the days he got right. The fear itself is old. In 1942, Walter Cannon argued that belief in a curse could kill through fear alone. Then the replies change. One says they didn't think there were others like them. Another notes his blog takes anonymous comments. The last says: I left him one. Eight experiments. Three successes. One comment, still waiting for an answer.

**Length:** about 57 s at the skill's 3.7 words a second, but about 73 s at the Joe speed we measured on ep 1 (2.87). To hit 60 s we either speed Joe up (a faster setting, tested on your PC) or cut the guesses line and the book line (about 12 s).
