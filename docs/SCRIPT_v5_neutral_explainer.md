# Episode 0 (pilot): "Anon Went Quiet" (v5, neutral explainer, awaiting approval)

Format: a calm, neutral explanation of one public /x/ thread (8 posts over about 2 h 43 min): what it is, what the poster claims, and what the replies discuss. It does not argue for or against the claims. The claims are always presented as his claims, never as fact.

Safeguards: no names, handles, usernames or links. Nothing that helps anyone find the blog or any person. No images copied. No slurs. Nothing about the child-related cases. No jokes about deaths. Post 6 (the gore joke) is not mentioned. The blog's specific "results" are not described.

Series cast: Professor Gloom narrates, calm, flashlight in hand. No reaction gags and no cat gag in this serious episode.
World: a dark "thread room". Posts are paper cards pinned to a corkboard with a time stamp (+0, +33 min and so on). Anons are faceless silhouettes at glowing monitors. Cold blue palette.
Captions: big, bold, 2 to 4 words at a time.
On screen at the start and the end: "Anonymous online claims. Unverified. No names. No links."

## Voice-over (~248 words, ~67 s)
**HOOK**
This is a thread from an anonymous message board. It's about a man who says he can harm people from a distance, and who hasn't posted in a week. Here is what the thread says.

**POST 1 · +0 min**
The original post notes that his last thread was archived. A week of silence, on the board and on his blog. The poster wonders if he's waiting for someone to start a thread about him.

**POST 2 · +33 min**
The first reply lists possible reasons. Real life getting in the way. No new results. Losing interest. Or outside interference. The replier hopes he comes back, and wishes he'd shared his method.

**POST 3 · +1 h**
The next reply gives the figure people keep returning to. According to the thread, he reported three successes out of eight experiments in October. The poster calls that a huge result, and compares it to hitting a one-metre target a kilometre away in a storm.

**POST 4 · +1 h 27**
A third reply says he kept talking about failure, as if he expected to succeed every time. It also mentions a book about psychic attacks on strangers, and wonders if his method is similar.

**POSTS 5, 7, 8 · +2 h 15 to +2 h 43**
Later, one anon writes that they hadn't thought there were others like them. Another notes that his blog accepts anonymous comments. A third says they left one.

**CLOSE**
That is the whole thread. It doesn't say what counted as a success, who was involved, or whether anything he describes happened. None of that can be checked from the thread. And as far as it shows, he never replied.

## Notes
- Every statement about him is attributed ("says", "reported", "according to the thread"). Nothing is stated as fact.
- One neutral line on verifiability is in the close, with no argument against the claims.
- The thread's own rifle analogy is reported as the replier's comparison and not examined.
