## Voice-over (the story, told out loud)

HOOK
A man online says he can curse strangers to death, and it only works when he stops trying.

CLAIM
He posted in a Death Note fan thread, and his blog logs colds, missed buses, and, very rarely, death.

HOW IT SUPPOSEDLY WORKS
His best method, he says, is vulgar: a printed photo, an insult, the target's name, and pure hatred, with no thinking, just feeling.

EVIDENCE
He ran it like a lab: forty subjects, each matched with a control he never touched, every target coded in advance.

One reply says October gave him three hits out of eight: like hitting a one-metre target at a thousand metres, in a storm.

THE TURN
Then he went silent for a week.

CREEPY TURN
Another anon wrote, "I didn't think there were others like me." His blog takes anonymous comments. Someone left one.

CONTEXT (the "You see" pivot)
You see, the idea is old. In 1942, Walter Cannon argued that belief in a curse can kill through fear alone.

OPEN, UNVERIFIED END
Nobody can say who the targets were, or what counts as a hit. None of it can be verified.
