# Episode 0 (pilot): "Anon Went Quiet" (v3, follows the thread post by post, awaiting approval)

Source: one public /x/ thread, 8 posts (the original post plus 7 replies) over about 2 h 43 min, still live when read on 2026-11-25. Everything the thread says is an unverified anonymous claim. The video never names the poster or his blog, uses no usernames or post numbers, and copies no images. Each beat below shows the thread post it covers and what the animation shows.

Series cast: Professor Gloom (narrator, flashlight), Moth (nervous reader), Biscuit the cat (background gag).
World: a dark "thread room". Posts are big paper cards pinned to a corkboard wall with string, each stamped with its time gap (+0, +33 min, ...). Anons are faceless silhouettes at glowing monitors. Gloom points his flashlight at each card as it is read; Moth reads over his shoulder.

## Voice-over
**HOOK** (cards flying onto the wall)
A man claimed he could hurt people with his mind. Then he stopped posting. So the board investigated. Eight posts. Under three hours.

**POST 1 · +0 min** (card 1, empty chair, an archive box)
The original post. His last thread got archived. A week of silence, here and on his blog. Maybe he's waiting for someone to make a thread about him. Congratulations. Someone did.

**POST 2 · +33 min** (card 2, four thought-bubble theories)
Reply one lists theories. Real life. No results, so he quit. Bored of role-playing. Or, quote, "the glowies found him." The classic.

**POST 3 · +1 h** (card 3, tally board 3 / 8, then a tiny target far away in a storm)
Reply two has the numbers. October: eight experiments, three successes. Massive, says one anon, like hitting a one-metre target a kilometre away in a storm. Then the real question: is it the hit rate, or the fact that he owns a rifle at all?

**POST 4 · +1 h 27** (card 4, a book on a desk, Moth recoils)
Reply three notices he kept talking about failure, like he expected perfect scores. Then a book comes up, about psychic attacks on strangers. Not meant to kill, apparently. Just to ruin your week. One anon replied with a joke we can't repeat here.

**POSTS 5, 7, 8 · +2 h 15 to +2 h 43** (three small cards in a row, a blog window glows, a hand drops a note into it)
Then it gets quiet. One anon didn't think there were others like them. Another notes his blog takes anonymous comments. The last one says: I left him a comment.

**DEADPAN** (Gloom turns to camera)
Luckily, nobody has proved he hurt anyone. Unluckily, nobody has proved he didn't.

**EXPLANATION · our analysis, not the thread's** (night blue, from "You see")
You see, the thread never says what a success was, or who the targets were. Three out of eight sounds like dark magic, until you remember you only hear about the hits.

**CLOSE** (empty chair, Biscuit blinks)
As far as the thread shows, he never replied. Sleep well.
