# Episode pitches, batch 2

All eight are about objects, events, places or internet fiction. None involve a private person or a real death. Facts below are from memory and NOT yet verified. Each gets sourced at the script stage. Tone is a suggestion.

| # | Story | Hook | Tone | Risk |
|---|-------|------|------|------|
| 10 | **Kryptos** | A sculpture outside the CIA has a message. Three parts are solved. The fourth, 97 characters, isn't. | informational | Low. Don't state solutions as certain beyond what's published. |
| 11 | **The Backrooms** | An image posted on an anonymous board in 2019 became a whole genre of "liminal" horror. | funny | Low. Credit the origin generally. No board links. Check later spin-off claims. |
| 12 | **The Buzzer (UVB-76)** | A Russian radio station has buzzed since the 1970s, with rare spoken messages. | informational | Low to medium. Say "reported", and don't say who runs it. |
| 13 | **The Tully Monster** | A fossil from Illinois that scientists still argue about. | funny | Very low. Check the latest classification. |
| 14 | **Racetrack Playa's sailing stones** | Rocks in Death Valley move on their own and leave trails. Now explained. | funny | Very low. Verify the 2014 study details. |
| 15 | **Candle Cove** | A "lost" kids' TV show, remembered in a creepypasta that fooled people. | funny | Low. It's openly fiction, so say who wrote it. |
| 16 | **Oak Island Money Pit** | A pit in Nova Scotia that treasure hunters have dug at for over 200 years. | informational | Medium. Skip the claimed deaths unless well sourced. |
| 17 | **The Hum** | Some people in some towns hear a low hum that others don't. | informational | Low. Say "reported" for all causes. |

Held back: Zodiac-type cases and any story with an identifiable victim or perpetrator.
