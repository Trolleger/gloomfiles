# Ep 11 source digest: The Backrooms
Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Backrooms (read 2026-11-26; body verbatim to ~75%).
1. May 12, 2019: an anonymous /x/ thread asks for "disquieting images that just feel 'off'"; a photo: carpeted empty room, yellow wallpaper, fluorescent lights, tilted angle.
2. A reply named it and described it: "the stink of old moist carpet", "the madness of mono-yellow", "the endless background noise of fluorescent lights at maximum hum-buzz", "approximately six hundred million square miles of randomly segmented empty rooms".
3. "God save you if you hear something wandering around nearby, because it sure as hell has heard you."
4. Origin unknown until May 2024: a Discord community traced it on the Wayback Machine to a March 2003 page.
5. The photo: June 12, 2002, Sony Cyber-shot, file Dsc00161.jpg, the upstairs "East (Oval) room" of a hobby shop under renovation, 807 Oregon Street, Oshkosh, Wisconsin; no windows, water damage. Earlier a furniture store; now an RC car track; the room is gone.
6. Fans added "levels" and "entities"; r/backrooms 157,000+ members by March 2022; wikis work like the SCP wiki.
7. Liminal spaces: usually-busy places shown empty; a sense of "having been there before"; #liminalspaces ~100 million TikTok views.
8. Critics: "a fungal, sickly yellow" (Paste); "an uncanny valley of place" (PC Gamer).
9. 2022: TikTok trend searching Google Earth for "entrances".
10. Jan 2022: Kane Parsons (Kane Pixels), 16, uploads "The Backrooms (Found Footage)", VHS style, made in Blender in about a month; 78 million+ views.
11. A24 announced his film Feb 6, 2023; released in the US May 29, 2026.
12. Games: one two months after the post; Escape the Backrooms; poolrooms (endless white-tiled pools).
13. Severance's creator cited it as an influence.
