# Ep 15 source digest: Candle Cove
Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candle_Cove (read 2026-11-26, full article text).
1. Written by web cartoonist and author Kris Straub, published 2009 on his site ichorfalls.com (Creative Commons), part of his stories about the fictional town Ichor Falls.
2. Format: a thread on a fictional forum, "NetNostalgia Forums"; users from the Huntington-Ashland area reminisce.
3. The show: "Candle Cove", a kids' programme on Channel 58 in the early 1970s; a girl named Janice and her imaginary adventures with marionette pirates.
4. Memories get darker: a villain, the "Skin-Taker", a skeleton pirate wearing clothes made of children's skin (gore: nod only); an episode that was only the characters screaming.
5. Ending: a user tells of asking his mother; she said whenever he said he was watching Candle Cove, the TV was only showing static. (Wikipedia paraphrases, no quote.)
6. Inspiration (Straub, 2011 interview): an Onion article, "Area 36-Year-Old Still Has Occasional Lidsville Nightmare".
7. Spread: copy-pasted across creepypasta.com, the Creepypasta Wiki, 4chan, YouTube, IGN, horror.com; on some forums fans re-staged the conversation as if real.
8. 2015: self-published in "Candle Cove and Other Stories". The Verge: unlike most creepypastas' "anonymous folkloric quality", this one had a known author.
9. Syfy adapted it as season 1 of Channel Zero (greenlit Nov 18, 2015); premiered Oct 11, 2016 (Paul Schneider, Fiona Shaw): a child psychologist returns home to investigate 1980s disappearances of his brother and other children.
10. Spinoff named: Local 58 (analog horror).
11. The Verge: "a perfectly dark spin on our nostalgia for the half-remembered stories of our childhood." Aeon: "among the best creepypastas out there".
12. Academic paper: Balanzategui, "Creepypasta, 'Candle Cove', and the digital gothic", Journal of Visual Culture, 2019.
