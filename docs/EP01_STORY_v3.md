# EP01 "Anon Went Quiet": story script v3 + world shot list (for Andrew's OK)

> Superseded by EP01_STORY_v4.md (Andrew: bring Death Note back, use the thread's real details).

Written 2026-11-26 to the gloom-files-script and gloom-files-visuals skills. Replaces section 3 (script) and section 4 (shots) of EP01_PLAN_v2. Style, music, process and gates in EP01_PLAN_v2 still apply. Nothing is built or rendered.

## What changed
v2 read like a log of the forum thread ("eight posts, two hours forty-three", "archived", "replies guess"). v3 tells the story of one man, on the skill's arc: **hook → his claim → how it supposedly works → his score → the silence → the creepy turn → the history behind the idea → open, unverified ending**. The thread is only mentioned as the place where people noticed he was gone.

Same facts as v6 and the same safeguards: every claim is attributed ("he says", "one reader"), no names, handles, links or blog content, nothing says he hurt anyone, and the disclaimer card (Anonymous online claims. Unverified. No names. No links.) stays at the start and end. Gloom only, no gags.

## Script (167 spoken words, about 58 s at the fast Joe pace)
| # | Arc step | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | An anonymous man online says he can attack strangers with his mind. Then he stopped posting. |
| 2 | Claim | He called them psychic experiments, and wrote about them on his blog. |
| 3 | How it supposedly works | One reader traces his method to a paperback, The Forbidden Parapsychology, sold as techniques for influencing people from a distance. |
| 4 | Evidence | In October, another reader counted his results. Eight experiments. Three successes. |
| 5 | Evidence, scaled | Huge, they wrote, like hitting a one-metre target at a thousand metres in a storm. |
| 6 | What happened | Then, a week of silence, on the paranormal board and on his blog. People started asking where he went. Real life? The feds? |
| 7 | Creepy turn | And it got stranger. One said they didn't think there were others like them. Another noticed his blog takes anonymous comments. A third said they'd left one. |
| 8 | Context | The idea is old. In 1930, occultist Dion Fortune blamed her own breakdown on a psychic attack. In 1942, physiologist Walter Cannon argued belief in a curse could kill by fear alone. |
| 9 | Open ending | He never replied. No target named, no success defined. Nothing anyone can verify. |

**Cut from v6 (say if any must come back, about 3 s each):** the /x/ history (2005 photography board, 2007 switch), the thread length and post times, the Death Note title reference, "his last thread was archived", the boredom and no-results guesses, "wishes he'd published his method", the book's year and page count, "he keeps writing about failure, as if he expected to win every time", the replier's "not meant to kill" wording, the title of Fortune's book, the "later researchers" line.
