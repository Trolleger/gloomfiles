# Shot plans for the Remotion build (episodes 1 to 9)

Nothing here is built yet. Each plan maps the approved script beats to scenes, timed to about 60 s. Final timing is set by the Piper voice-over.

## Rule (Andrew, 2026-11-25): depict, don't narrate
Every line of voice-over must be SHOWN in a 3D scene built from the prop library (remotion/PROPS.md). Gloom, Moth and Biscuit are guides inside the scene, never a cast member talking in an unrelated room. All plans below were checked against this rule; ep 1 and 2 are built, eps 3-9 compose from the library (missing props listed in PROPS.md).

## Shared style (all episodes)
- Canvas 1080x1920, 30 fps. Render only when Andrew says (about 90 min each on his PC).
- Cast per `cast-concept-v1.png`: Professor Gloom (bald, round glasses, dark purple suit, red tie, flashlight), Moth (teal shirt, tuft, nervous), Biscuit (black cat, yellow eyes). Navy background, soft vignette.
- Funny episodes use all three. Informational episodes use Gloom only, and in a neutral setting.
- Safe area for all three apps: keep captions and key text inside x 80 to 940, y 260 to 1450. The apps overlay buttons and captions on the right edge and bottom, so nothing important in the bottom 450 px or the right 140 px. These numbers are approximate. Test on the real overlays once.
- Captions: big, bold, 2 to 4 words at a time, high contrast, numbers always on screen.
- Start with a 1-second hook frame. Cut roughly every 4 to 6 s. End on the closing line, then 1 s of Biscuit or a title card.
- Audio: Piper voice (en_US-joe-medium or en_US-norman-medium), synthesized or royalty-free low music bed, one sound hit per scene change.
