# The Man Who Stopped Posting (draft v1, ~60 s)

Framing: an internet mystery about an anonymous forum poster. No names, no blog URL, no targets, no claim that any of it is true.

## Facts (source: one /x/ thread, 7 replies, read 2026-11-25; the claims are unverified)
- An anonymous poster claimed to be able to harm people "psychically".
- He reportedly logged 8 experiments in October and called 3 of them successes.
- He never shared his method and wrote about "failure" as if he expected none.
- He went quiet for a week, and his thread was archived.
- Other posters guessed: real life, boredom, or the feds.
- A reply said they "didn't think there were others like me".

## Voice-over
**Hook:** An anonymous man online said he could hurt strangers with his mind. Then he stopped posting.

**Setting:** October. A forum for people who think they have powers. One of them starts a blog.

**Action:**
He logs his experiments. Eight tries. Three successes.
He never says what a success is.
He never says how he did it.
And he writes about failure like it's something that was never supposed to happen.

**Snap:** Then, nothing. A week of silence. His thread gets archived.

**Deadpan double line:** Luckily, nobody can prove he did anything. Unluckily, nobody can prove he didn't.

**Explanation:** You see, here's the trick. Three hits out of eight sounds like magic, until you remember you only hear about the hits. It's like wishing a red light turns green. Lights turn green all day, and nobody counts the wishes that did nothing.

**Consequence:** The forum keeps asking one question. If he was real, where did he go? And if he was fake, why does everyone keep checking?

**Callback twist:** Someone left a comment on his blog. It said: "Didn't think there were others like me."

Nobody has replied.
