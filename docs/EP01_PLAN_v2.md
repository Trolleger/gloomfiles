# Gloom Files EP01 "Anon Went Quiet": plan v2 (for Andrew's approval, nothing is built from this yet)

## 0. What changed and why
The first rebuild stitched colour variants of one room. This plan starts from Andrew's style answers, a study of the reference channels, and the skill's process, and asks for approval before anything is built.

## 1. References studied
- **Retroyap** (watched "He stole fire from the Gods", 4.7M views, frame by frame in the browser). Notes:
  - Low-poly 3D, a **new place about every 4 s**: Zeus on a cloud throne, a snowy village seen from above, the forge of Hephaestus, then a close-up of a man holding a giant glowing fennel stalk.
  - Wide establishing shot, then a close-up. Objects exaggerated large. Strong warm vs cool contrast per place (blue snow vs orange forge).
  - One chunky coloured caption (2 to 3 words, centre-low) that changes every phrase; a small white subtitle sits at the top.
  - Characters are props in the world; the **world is the star**.
- **ZachDFilms**: the channel page would not load in my browser, so I have not studied it yet. I will not claim its style until I have seen it. If you can name one short, I'll watch it.
- **The skill's 20 reference shorts** (already studied): painterly toy-diorama worlds, ink lines, saturated colour with blue shadows, gliding camera with push-ins, kicks on impacts.

## 2. Style guide (from Andrew's answers)
1. **Painterly toy-diorama** with sketchy hand-drawn ink lines (skill ink pass, slightly wobblier line).
2. **Colour follows the topic**: cold violet for the mystery, warm amber for old books and candles, sickly green for the lab, storm blue for the range.
3. **Surreal dream-space worlds**, not plain rooms: floating islands, giant objects, impossible scale (a giant corkboard in the sky, a book that is a landscape, a clock the size of a building).
4. **Punchy camera**: fast pushes and zooms on key words, impact shakes (flip-sign, 7 frames), glides of 18 to 40 frames, no static hold over 4 frames.
5. **Gloom stays off to the side**, small, one fixed mark per shot with acting (point, lean, look, raised flashlight, expression change). The world is the star. Moth and Biscuit only as rare gags. Never glued to the camera.
6. **Captions**: big, 2 to 4 words, the **key word in colour** (yellow, red for danger words, mint for the claim). No other text except what the story names (book cover, clock 2:43, "3 of 8").
7. **A new place every 4 to 6 s**, hard cuts on big jumps, 6 to 8 frame dissolve only where time passes.
8. **Must not look AI-made**: hand-built props with deliberate silhouettes, no repeated clone rooms, no random glow clutter, each place designed for its line.
