[gloom] A low sound that some people hear, and others never do.
[gloom] The Hum is a persistent low-frequency drone reported in towns around the world. Only some people nearby can hear it.
[gloom] In the early 1990s, a survey in Taos, New Mexico, found about 2 percent of residents were hearers. Their descriptions varied. Instruments in their homes found nothing unusual.
[gloom] A 1995 study reported hearers heard it at different frequencies, between 32 and 80 hertz.
[gloom] Some hums were traced. Windsor, Ontario, had reports from 2011. They were linked to blast furnaces on a nearby island, and faded after they shut down in April 2020. In Kokomo, Indiana, two industrial sources were found. Reports continued after they were fixed.
[gloom] Other theories: tinnitus, unusually sensitive hearing, or focusing on ordinary sounds. One researcher estimated about a third of cases were physical and two thirds were not.
[gloom] There isn't one Hum. There are several, and some have no known cause.
