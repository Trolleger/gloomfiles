[gloom] 2009. A short story appears online, written as a forum thread. Adults trade memories of a children's show called Candle Cove, on Channel 58, in the early 1970s.
[gloom] The show: a girl named Janice and her imaginary adventures with marionette pirates.
[moth] I don't remember it at all.
[gloom] Then the memories turn strange. A skeleton pirate villain. An episode where the puppets only scream.
[gloom] The ending: one man says his mother told him that when he watched Candle Cove, the TV just showed static.
[gloom] It's fiction, by a web cartoonist named Kris Straub. People copied it onto forums, and some fans staged the conversation as though it had really happened. A TV series followed in 2016.
[gloom] There was never a show. There was only static. Gloom: "Which is also the case with most daytime TV."
