[gloom] Carbon dating in 2009 put the parchment between 1404 and 1438. That dates the material, not the writing.

[gloom] The text uses an unknown alphabet of roughly 20 to 25 main characters. Around 38,000 words, about 8,000 to 9,000 different ones.
[gloom] It behaves like language. Word frequencies follow the same pattern as real languages. But it also has odd traits, like repeated words and a very predictable structure.
[gloom] Theories: a cipher, an unknown language, an invented language, a hoax. Codebreakers from both world wars tried. None of the proposed solutions is accepted.
[gloom] It might say something. It might say nothing. Nobody has been able to prove which.
