[gloom] A fossil so strange scientists still can't agree what it was.
[gloom] 1955, Illinois. An amateur collector finds a fossil and takes it to the Field Museum. The experts are stumped.
[moth] A bit of a theme.
[gloom] It's about 300 million years old. Up to 35 centimetres long. A cigar-shaped body, a tail fin, and eyes on two stalks. And a long snout with a tiny claw at the end.
[gloom] It was named in 1966. In 1989, Illinois made it the state fossil.
[gloom] In 2016, two papers said it was a vertebrate, related to fish like lampreys. In 2017, another team replied that it was not.
[gloom] Since then: chemistry studies lean toward a chordate. A 2023 scan study doubts several of the vertebrate features. Still unsettled.
[gloom] Thousands of fossils. One verdict, missing. Gloom: "Science is thorough. Eventually."
